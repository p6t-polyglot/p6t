p6t automates the localization (l10n) workflow, so that your CD pipeline stays continuous. Wrap your strings in `<fbt>` components and ship every feature in every language—automatically.

{/*
here should be a way to quickly grasp the concept:

1. change text
2. hit save/commit/push
3. auto-translation runs
4. translations are updated (purging of the old, translations of the new are added)
5. new translations are available in the app immediately, no release blocking, no orphaned strings

*/}


Built on [fbtee](https://github.com/nkzw-tech/fbtee), the open-source successor to Facebook's fbt with over a decade of production support for billions of users.
